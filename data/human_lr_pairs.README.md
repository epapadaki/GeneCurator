# human_lr_pairs.tsv

**Source:** CellTalkDB (Shao et al., Briefings in Bioinformatics 2020)
**Accessed via:** LewisLabUCSD Ligand-Receptor-Pairs compendium
https://github.com/LewisLabUCSD/Ligand-Receptor-Pairs

**Citation:**
Shao X, et al. CellTalkDB: a manually curated database of ligand-receptor
interactions in humans and mice. Brief Bioinform. 2021;22(4):bbaa269.
doi:10.1093/bib/bbaa269

**Content:** 3,398 human ligand-receptor pairs with HGNC gene symbols
and PubMed evidence IDs.

**Columns:**
- ligand: HGNC symbol of the ligand
- receptor: HGNC symbol of the receptor
- evidence: PubMed ID(s) supporting the pair

**License:** Academic use permitted per CellTalkDB terms.

**Usage in GeneCurator:** annotation only — flagging which panel genes are
known ligands or receptors and identifying complete LR pairs. Not used for
cell-cell communication inference.
